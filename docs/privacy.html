Privacy Policy

Updated November 9, 2026

Vihari doesn’t collect your data.

There’s no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your cities and alarms stay on your devices and in your own iCloud account.

What’s Stored and Where

The cities you pin, your alarms and your preferences are saved on your device. If you turn on iCloud Sync, they’re also kept in your iCloud account so your other devices can show them. They’re protected by your Apple Account and handled under Apple’s privacy policy; the developer can’t see them.

The latest forecast for each pinned city is saved on your device, so weather appears quickly and while you’re offline. It’s replaced as new forecasts arrive and removed when you unpin the city.

Weather

To get forecasts, Vihari sends the map coordinates of each city you pin to Open-Meteo, an open weather service, over an encrypted connection. It sends a city’s coordinates, never your location. As with any internet request, Open-Meteo can see your device’s IP address. Open-Meteo’s use of that information is covered by its own terms.

Open-Meteo Terms and Privacy

Maps and Search

The globe and city search use Apple Maps. Searches and the map areas being shown are sent to Apple to return results and imagery, under Apple’s privacy policy. Vihari doesn’t use or ask for your location.

Apple Privacy Policy

Alarms, Siri and Shortcuts

Alarms are scheduled with the system on your device. When you ask Siri or run a shortcut, Vihari answers on your device using your pinned cities; Siri requests are handled by Apple.

Diagnostics

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device’s settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with the developer that don’t identify you. You can change this at any time in Settings.

Your Choices

You can unpin cities and delete alarms at any time. Settings > Delete All Data removes everything Vihari has stored and, with iCloud Sync on, removes it from your other devices too. Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device.

Children

Vihari is suitable for everyone and doesn’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here and in the app, with a new date at the top.
